# Community Code of Conduct — Draft

> **Draft for maintainer review; not yet an adopted project policy.** The repository does not identify a project-specific reporting contact or enforcement process. The guidelines and moderation approach below are proposals, not existing commitments. Maintainers should review them and add a private reporting route before presenting this document as an adopted policy.

## Proposed community standards

We aim for project spaces—including issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related discussions—to be welcoming and constructive. Participants are asked to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing.
- Give technical feedback that is specific, relevant, and focused on the work rather than the person.
- Welcome questions and differing experience levels; assume good intent while addressing the effect of harmful behavior.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's private information or expose credentials, tokens, or other secrets.

The following conduct is not acceptable under this draft: harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, or sharing private information without consent. Criticism of an idea or implementation is welcome when expressed respectfully and on topic.

## Reporting and response — maintainer decision needed

No private conduct-reporting contact is specified in the repository at the time of this draft. **Before adopting this policy, maintainers should publish a private reporting method and identify who will receive reports.** Do not put sensitive allegations or personal information in a public issue or pull-request comment. If a report cannot be handled safely through a published project contact, use GitHub's own reporting options for content that violates its terms.

### Proposed moderation approach

For maintainer review, a possible response is to address reported behavior promptly and proportionately, considering context and impact. Depending on the circumstances, maintainers could edit or remove content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation. This is a draft suggestion, not a promise that the project currently has a particular enforcement or appeal process.

## Adoption checklist for maintainers

Before removing the draft label, maintainers should decide and document:

1. The private channel for reports and who monitors it.
2. The moderation and escalation process, including how decisions are communicated.
3. Whether and how an affected participant may request review of a moderation decision.
4. Whether the proposed standards and responses should be changed for this project.
